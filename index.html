Strain model and thresholds

Strain is a point score: hours door to door, connections, time-zone shift, red-eyes, and turnarounds shorter than your buffer. Under 15 is light, under 35 moderate, under 60 heavy, above that brutal. Tune it to how you actually feel after a trip.

How routing works. Between two committed events, you either go home (two legs) or hop straight to the next city (one leg). Gaps of at least the "go home" threshold default to home; shorter gaps default to the direct hop. Override any gap in the routing table.

Where prices come from. Expedia quotes use your own Expedia connector on claude.ai and store the cheapest one-way fare for that exact day; fares move, so re-quote before you book. Nothing here books anything.

Where this is saved. Inside claude.ai the plan and quotes sync to your account so you can open it from any device. Opened anywhere else, it stays in this browser only.
